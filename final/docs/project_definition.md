# TalentRoute project definition after Intermediate feedback

A university learner-support coordinator working with an educational consulting team decides which course-enrolment cases to review, which to alert, and which receive no action under limited staff time. OULAD supports historical outcome-targeting analysis; it contains neither measured intervention benefits nor an observed staffing workflow. Costs and action rules are declared scenarios.

Classification and engagement outlier detection are the two connected technique families. A bounded grid of logistic, decision-tree and boosting candidates estimate Success/Fail/Withdrawn. Isolation Forest supplies a separate reserved review queue. The final choice uses three learner-grouped folds within 2013B and excludes all assessment-derived features. All final-prefixed analyses and the default app use this assessment-free model. Earlier assessment-enabled outputs remain historical comparisons. The reserve is carved from the same review capacity; it is not added to the budget or mixed numerically with outcome probability. Removing it gives the classifier-only comparison. Inactivity and seeded random reserves test whether sophistication earns its place.

Preserved commitments:

1. Equal-quota reconciliation: interleave classifier, detector and inactivity lists at the same total contact quota, measuring gained/lost coverage by course.
2. Warning-positive profiles: compare later Success/Fail/Withdrawn among flagged cases, keeping observed-score denominators. No resilience or intervention-effect claim.
3. Course segments and specialists: train only where the historical training sample supports it, fall back globally otherwise, and evaluate seen/unseen courses and returning learners separately.

The primary ML metric remains adverse-outcome average precision. The primary decision metric remains adverse coverage at the common workload. Action-specific targeting counts, contact precision, effort and conditional preference regions address the limitations of coverage. Original detector acceptance required positive paired coverage improvement with no more than a small stated precision loss; this is an exploratory criterion, not permission to deploy.

The availability audit motivates an earlier-only training scenario and both identity-disjoint and operational training companions. This changes population and training support, so new metrics are not direct improvements on submitted numbers. The historical final cohort has been examined; this revision does not score it. A revised final confirmation protocol is still unresolved. Detailed temporal and methodological boundaries: development_protocol.md. Executed results and remaining gaps: final_report.md.
