# Independent confirmation: status and future protocol

## What is available now

Earlier project records used 2013B and 2013J for training, 2014B for development and 2014J for a historical final evaluation. Subsequent development followed those results. The current calendar-aware revision trains on 2013B and evaluates on already examined 2014B. No new cohort is available as of 22 November 2026.

The current revision therefore has no independent final performance estimate. The historical 2014J result belongs to its original system, feature pipeline and selection history; it cannot be transferred to the revised system. Repeated splits, cross-validation and additional seeds measure aspects of internal stability but do not erase prior exposure. The final rubric's independent evaluation gap remains open.

## Protocol when genuinely new data become available

Before seeing outcomes, establish permission, compatible course context, real outcome definitions and the availability of each predictor at day 28. Do not invent labels or turn a different task's labels into OULAD outcomes. If fields or population differ materially, treat the exercise as a new transportability study with a separate development process.

For compatible data, preserve a dated code/configuration snapshot: the assessment-free classifier selected within three learner-grouped 2013B folds, risk-only routing, day-28 activity cutoff, the stated time budget and action costs. Exclude assessment-derived fields when score-release timing is unverified. Reintroducing those fields requires verified release timestamps and a separately frozen development specification. Retain the same baseline and the anomaly-reserve ablation. Freeze metrics and analysis before outcome access. Any changed features, thresholds or selection rule require a distinct development record.

Score eligible cases with information available at the decision time. Retain predictions and allocated actions before joining later real outcomes. Evaluate model discrimination and probability quality, action-specific targeting, staff workload, course and returning-learner segments, and paired learner-cluster uncertainty. Check actual cohort dates and learner overlap. Report a negative result rather than changing the method after examining the test. Software tests and a working demo are not substitutes for this experiment.

Independent targeting validation still would not establish intervention benefit. That requires observed support delivery, effort and outcomes under an appropriate prospective study design. No prospective collection, institutional deployment or instructor approval is claimed here.
